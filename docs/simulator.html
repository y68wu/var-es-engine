ABOUT THE DESK

Each replay contains 30 synthetic market sessions. The 3M European USD/CAD straddle uses the initial at-the-money strike. Each straddle contract covers USD 100,000 per leg. Sell to open or buy to cover.

The desk uses a fixed CAD 12,000 cash reserve per short contract and caps positions at five contracts. Fills omit fees and formal brokerage margin. Visit the model lab for the historical portfolio results and exact repricing curves.
