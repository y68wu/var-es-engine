04 / Data & method

From market close
to loss distribution.

The engine aligns daily market observations, builds a synthetic CAD portfolio and revalues it across five correlated risk factors.

01 / INPUTS

Align closes

XIU, USD/CAD, five GoC zero-curve knots, CORRA and US 3M Treasury yields.

02 / PRICING

Mark the book

Cash-flow bond pricing and analytical option Greeks. Use realized EWMA FX volatility as an implied-volatility proxy.

03 / VALIDATION

Test the tail

500-day estimation windows, 750 forecasts, and separate tests for coverage and exception clustering.
