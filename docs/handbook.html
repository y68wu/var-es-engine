Week 01 · Oct 11–Oct 17

Price the book

Define the exposures, align public observations and validate the instruments before estimating a loss distribution.

Day 01 Oct 11 · Sun

Define the reference book

Size the CAD 10M book at CAD 5M in XIU, CAD 3M in a 5Y GoC bond and CAD 2M in a 10Y GoC bond. Sell a 3M USD/CAD call and put at the same spot-ATM strike, with USD 15M notional per leg. Retain the premium as CAD cash.

Check. Check that the cash and option liability offset at inception and that opening NAV ties to CAD 10M.

Day 02 Oct 12 · Mon

Specify the input feeds

Map XIU and USD/CAD closes to Yahoo Finance, GoC zero yields and CORRA to the Bank of Canada, and the 3M U.S. Treasury rate to FRED. Set prices, rates and volatility to consistent units. Treat CAD per USD as the spot quote.

Check. Record series names, sample dates and a checksum for the locally downloaded panel.

Data dictionary ↗
Day 03 Oct 13 · Tue

Align closes without filling risky inputs

Intersect the observed equity, FX and GoC curve dates. Attach the latest strictly prior CORRA and DGS3MO observations within seven calendar days. Preserve the distinction between observation date and publication date for the lagged GoC curve.

Check. Use a small fixture with a missing close and same-day carry observations to verify the joint calendar and strictly prior rate matching.

Market alignment tests ↗
Day 04 Oct 14 · Wed

Build the FX volatility proxy

Compute USD/CAD log returns and seed daily variance with the first 30 squared returns. Update variance with 0.94 persistence and annualize with 252 sessions. Feed the resulting realized-volatility proxy to the option model.

Check. Check the seed and next recursive update by hand, and make sure later observations cannot change earlier volatility values.

EWMA implementation ↗
Day 05 Oct 15 · Thu

Implement European FX option pricing

Use Garman–Kohlhagen with the CAD proxy as domestic carry and the USD proxy as foreign carry. Price the call and put independently. Derive spot delta, gamma, vega and theta, with additional curvature terms for joint FX and volatility moves.

Check. Compare the four primary Greeks with finite differences. Verify put–call parity and intrinsic value at expiry.

FX pricing and Greeks ↗
Day 06 Oct 16 · Fri

Discount the two GoC cash-flow schedules

Construct semiannual coupons and principal for synthetic 5Y and 10Y bonds. Interpolate zero rates across the five available knots and discount each flow at its own tenor. Compute parallel DV01 and convexity, and isolate 5Y and 10Y key-rate exposures.

Check. Check that the par coupon prices each opening bond at face value. Bump the curve to verify the direction and magnitude of local rate sensitivities.

Position sensitivities ↗
Day 07 Oct 17 · Sat

Assemble daily marks and shock repricing

Combine equity, bonds, FX options and cash in one valuation interface. Keep position sizes fixed within a historical test. Reset the strike for each risk snapshot and hold the strike fixed when measuring the following shock.

Check. Compare the vectorized repricer against individual valuations. Keep daily snapshot marks separate from held one-day P&L with carry and elapsed time.

Week 02 · Oct 18–Oct 24

Estimate the tail

Build four model families on the same five-factor shock convention and isolate what changes when option curvature enters the valuation.

Day 08 Oct 18 · Sun

Define the shock vector and loss sign

Use log moves for XIU and USD/CAD, and absolute decimal changes for the 5Y and 10Y zero yields and FX volatility. Keep short-end curve knots, funding proxies and tenor fixed within modeled shocks. Express risk as positive CAD losses.

Check. Verify all five factor coordinates against finite differences of the portfolio, including the FX-volatility cross term.

Factor mapping and repricing ↗
Day 09 Oct 19 · Mon

Implement historical simulation

Revalue the held portfolio under each of the preceding 500 observed joint shock vectors. Read 99% VaR from the empirical loss quantile and compute 97.5% ES by averaging the upper tail with fractional mass where the cutoff crosses a sample point.

Check. Test fractional tail weighting and document that only about five observations lie beyond the historical 99% threshold.

Historical risk and tail tests ↗
Day 10 Oct 20 · Tue

Implement the delta-normal benchmark

Project the sample shock mean and covariance through the portfolio gradient. Use the resulting normal distribution for the loss quantile and tail average. This gives a baseline with the same calibration as Gaussian simulation.

Check. Compare with the closed form for a linear exposure. Add an at-zero-delta short-gamma case where a linear model misses the quadratic loss.

Delta-normal risk ↗
Day 11 Oct 21 · Wed

Add the delta-gamma approximation

Build a quadratic loss approximation from the gradient and Hessian. Compute its cumulants under Gaussian shocks and adjust the quantile with Cornish–Fisher. Sort the adjusted quantile grid when the approximation is nonmonotone.

Check. Use a noncentral chi-square case to check the quadratic cumulants with a nonzero shock mean. Check behavior when Cornish–Fisher needs rearrangement.

Delta-gamma and quantile tests ↗
Day 12 Oct 22 · Thu

Simulate Gaussian shocks

Correlate 10,000 standard normal draws with the Cholesky factor of the estimated covariance and apply full repricing to every path. Reuse seeded base draws for comparisons across dates and valuation methods.

Check. Check the fallback for a singular covariance matrix so deterministic factor directions retain zero variance.

Simulation engine ↗
Day 13 Oct 23 · Fri

Simulate joint Student-t shocks

Give the Gaussian innovations a common per-path Student-t scale with six degrees of freedom. Rescale to match the Gaussian covariance. Run the same full repricer so the comparison isolates the joint shock distribution.

Check. Verify the shared path scale and covariance adjustment. Label Gaussian and Student-t as two variants of one Monte Carlo model family.

Student-t scaling test ↗
Day 14 Oct 24 · Sat

Isolate the impact of the short straddle

Compare linear and full-revaluation VaR on identical Gaussian paths. Repeat with the same equity and bond book and the option position removed. The paired VaR shortfall is 2.6% with the straddle. Linear VaR is slightly higher in the control.

Check. Keep the paired result separate from the 4.3% gap between analytical delta-normal and simulated full revaluation.

Week 03 · Oct 25–Oct 31

Challenge the estimates

Run a strictly out-of-sample forecast loop, test coverage and clustering, then replay crisis shocks on the report-date portfolio.

Day 15 Oct 25 · Sun

Freeze the rolling forecast protocol

Backtest 750 realization dates from October 11, 2023 through October 21, 2026. For each date, fit all model variants to the preceding 500 aligned shocks. Form the portfolio snapshot from the prior observation, then score the next realized shock.

Check. Assert that the training slice excludes the realization. Count an exception only when the realized loss strictly exceeds forecast VaR.

Forecast timing tests ↗
Day 16 Oct 26 · Mon

Measure unconditional coverage

Count 99% VaR exceptions for each model and compare the observed frequency against 1% using the Kupiec likelihood ratio. Keep the count, rate, statistic and p-value in the same diagnostics table.

Check. Review the probability-boundary fixtures, including all-hit and no-hit sequences. Compare 12 delta-normal breaches with 8 historical simulation breaches.

Coverage diagnostics ↗
Day 17 Oct 27 · Tue

Test for clustered exceptions

Build the four no-hit/hit transition counts for each model. Apply the Christoffersen independence and conditional coverage tests to see whether exceedances arrive independently and at the target frequency.

Check. Use a fixture with equal exception counts but different ordering. In the empirical sample, all five variants flag clustering at 5%.

Transition and likelihood tests ↗
Day 18 Oct 28 · Wed

Apply the 250-day traffic light

Classify the final 250 forecasts using Basel-style bands of 0–4 green, 5–9 amber and at least 10 red. All five variants fall in the green band. Keep the full-period independence result visible alongside the traffic light.

Check. Check the four band boundaries and verify that earlier exceptions do not enter the final-250 count.

Traffic-light diagnostics ↗
Day 19 Oct 29 · Thu

Trace the nonlinear payoff

Hold other factors constant and sweep USD/CAD spot up and down. Plot exact, delta-only and delta-gamma P&L. At a +2% move, delta alone gives a CAD 69.7k gain while full repricing gives a CAD 73.7k loss.

Check. Check the zero-shock origin and show where a local quadratic begins to overstate the loss at larger moves.

Replay observed crises

Apply historical spot, rate and volatility changes since each episode’s preceding close to the same report-date book. Replay September–November 2008, March 2020 and calendar 2022 at fixed strike and tenor.

Check. Record end-of-period P&L and worst observed close separately. The 2008 replay’s worst close gives a CAD 6.16M loss.

Run individual and combined stresses

Bump the CAD curve and CAD option carry proxy by 200 bp, depreciate the CAD by 10% in USD terms, and double FX volatility. Apply the shocks separately and then reprice their combined effect.

Check. Translate the CAD depreciation to an 11.11% increase in USD/CAD. Reconcile total and position-level P&L for the CAD 2.51M combined loss.
